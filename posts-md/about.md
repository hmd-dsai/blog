I like building AI systems that solve real problems, and I'm curious about what goes on underneath them, especially whether and how language models actually reason. For now, I'm focused on getting hands-on experience as an AI engineer. Further down the road, I hope to pursue a master's degree in AI/NLP.

## What I'm doing now
- Leading the Developer Department and the Data & AI track at Google Developer Group on Campus - HUST (GDG-HUST).
- Surviving second year at HUST :>

## Things I've built
- **Agicom** (*GDGoC Hackathon Vietnam 2026, national champion*): a multi-agent AI system that helps small e-commerce shops handle customer service, content and risk. *Read the story:* [*How we went from eliminated to champion*](https://hmd-dsai.github.io/blog/20260621-gdgoc-hackathon-champion/).
- **Skillora** (*GDG-HUST Internal Hackathon 2026, champion*): a platform that analyzes students' GitHub repositories and turns their skills into a personalized skill tree.
- **Vietnamese clinical concept extraction** (*Viettel AI Race 2026*): a pipeline that pulls medical concepts out of Vietnamese clinical notes and maps them to standard medical codes.
- **RAG for multiple-choice QA** (*VNPT AI Hackathon 2025*): a retrieval pipeline that helps an LLM answer questions across many domains.
